## Consistent with existing rules (no change needed)

- Post-completion OPT: at least 20 hrs/week, directly related to the degree; self-employment possible on regular OPT.
- STEM OPT: E-Verify employer, training plan with employer evaluations, full-time (one 40-hr job or two 20-hr jobs), competitive pay; hard for solo founders.
- H-1B: employer registers in a March lottery for an October 1 start; wage-level-weighted selection; cap-exempt employers (universities, affiliated nonprofits) skip the lottery; concurrent cap-exempt H-1B is a known founder route.
